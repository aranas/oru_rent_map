Welcome to the Oxford Licence Map

This interactive map shows Oxford City Council's HMO and Selective Licence registers across the city's neighbourhoods (LSOAs). Open the ☰ Map layers menu (top-right) to toggle markers, agent highlights, and density maps.

Map elements
  • Blue markers — HMO licences (pre-loaded from Oxford City Council register).
  • Green markers — Selective licences (pre-loaded from Oxford City Council register).
  • Combined density — neighbourhood shading by total licence count. Toggle HMO-only or Selective-only density in the Map layers menu.
Data sources
  • OCC Licence data — Oxford City Council HMO Register and Selective Licence Register (public data).
  • OSM Map tiles & building footprints — OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • ONS Neighbourhood boundaries — LSOA polygons from the ONS Open Geography Portal.
Tips
  • ⓘ Hover over markers to see the full address, agent/holder name, and licence dates. If the neighbourhood shading makes markers hard to click, untick the density layer in the Map layers menu.

All reference data (tiles, boundaries, building footprints) is either served as static files or loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers.
