Welcome to the Oxford Licence Map

This interactive map shows Oxford City Council's HMO and Selective Licence registers across the city's neighbourhoods (LSOAs). Use the layer controls (top-right) to toggle markers and density maps. You can also upload your own CSV in the panel at the bottom-left to overlay additional data.

Map elements
  • Blue markers — HMO licences (pre-loaded from Oxford City Council register).
  • Green markers — Selective licences (pre-loaded from Oxford City Council register).
  • Combined density — neighbourhood shading by total licence count. Toggle HMO-only or Selective-only density in the layer control.
  • 🚪 Doorknock streets (Cowley) — optional overlay for canvassing: the Cowley streets (not neighbouring Rose Hill/Littlemore/Blackbird Leys/St Clement's) with the highest renter density and top-rental-agency listing density (per 100m, not raw totals — so a long road doesn't win just for having more addresses), with one overall meeting point (🚩) and a door/marker count panel per street.
Data sources
  • OCC Licence data — Oxford City Council HMO Register and Selective Licence Register (public data).
  • OSM Map tiles & building footprints — OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • ONS Neighbourhood boundaries — LSOA polygons from the ONS Open Geography Portal.
Tips
  • ⓘ Hover over markers to see the full address, agent/holder name, and licence dates. If the neighbourhood shading makes markers hard to click, untick the density layer in the top-right control.

All reference data (tiles, boundaries, building footprints) is either served as static files or loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers. No uploaded data leaves your browser.
